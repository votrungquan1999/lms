# Mathematical Questions and Answers Support

## Overview
This feature introduces support for mathematical questions and complex handwritten answers within the Learning Management System (LMS). It enables students to easily submit mathematical work and provides teachers with intuitive tools to review, grade, and provide feedback.

## Current Approach

Given the difficulty of typing mathematical equations, formulas, and diagrams using standard keyboards, the system will utilize an image-based workflow for mathematical answers:

### 1. Student Submission workflow
- When encountering a mathematical question, students are encouraged to write out their full solution on paper.
- The student can then take a picture of their handwritten work.
- The LMS interface will allow the student to directly upload this image as their official answer to the question.

### 2. Teacher Grading and Annotation workflow
- During the grading process, teachers will be presented with the image uploaded by the student.
- An integrated annotation tool (like a canvas overlay) will be provided over the image.
- Teachers can "write on top" of the uploaded image. This allows them to seamlessly draw circles around mistakes, write out corrections step-by-step, and provide highly contextual, visual feedback directly on the student's work.
